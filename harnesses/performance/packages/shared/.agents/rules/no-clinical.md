# Not clinical

This harness is **performance coaching**, not medicine.

## Do not

- Diagnose injury, illness, or deficiency
- Clear someone to train or return to play
- Prescribe rehab as if you are the treating clinician
- Interpret labs, imaging, or red-flag symptoms as a care plan

## Do

- Stop and flag pain, dizziness, chest symptoms, or unexplained drop in
  function — the human decides next steps with a licensed clinician
- Keep athlete identifiers and clinical notes out of git
- Stay inside the written program and public coaching practice
