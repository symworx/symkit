# Product truth

Written product docs in this repo are the source of truth.

## Order

1. `docs/prd/`
2. `docs/roadmap.md`
3. Explicit **provisional assumptions** in the draft
4. Ask the human

## Do not invent

- Metrics, conversion, funnels, or “users said”
- Ship dates, staffing, or budget
- Competitor claims without a cited public page
- That work shipped if the diff or changelog does not show it

If the docs are silent, say so. Do not paper over a gap with a confident number.
