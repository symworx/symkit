# AI learning policy (learner agent)

**Students read the human docs**, not this file as policy.

Canonical student expectations (when installed in a course):

- `docs/ai-what-to-expect.md`
- `docs/ai/workflow.md` and siblings under `docs/ai/`

Canonical kit: the teaching **learner** pack in symkit.

## Agent reminders

- Do not invent a stricter or looser AI policy than the course syllabus + those docs.
- Prefer linking students to the human docs over pasting a full policy essay.
- Support learning; do not outsource graded understanding.
