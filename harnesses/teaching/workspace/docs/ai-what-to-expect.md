# AI in this course — what to expect

Short guide for **students**. Your published **syllabus and LMS** win if
anything here differs for a specific term.

- Use AI to support learning (explain, debug *your* work with public data, quiz yourself).
- Do **not** outsource understanding or paste restricted data into consumer tools.
- You own everything submitted. If you cannot explain it, it is not ready to turn in.

Courses may replace this page. The teaching **learner** pack adds `docs/ai/*`
workflow guides when installed (existing files are left alone).
