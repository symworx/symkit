# Comedy harness

Bits from a written premise. The comedian is the session. Dadbot is the
non-vulgar dad-joke leaf.

```bash
./cli/symkit init /path/to/bits \
  --harness comedy --role comedian --scaffold
```

Roles: `comedian`, `materials`.

`comedian` installs `comedian` and `dadbot`. That session may spawn
dadbot for puns and groaners. Stories, characters, act-outs, and tags
stay with the comedian.

There is no `dadbot` role. Dadbot does not tell vulgar jokes and does
not take over the set.

`docs/room/`, `bits/`, and the human's notes are the record. Do not
invent laughs or that a bit was performed.
