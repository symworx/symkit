# Bits as truth

The premise, `docs/room/`, the bits in the repo, and the human's notes
are the record.

## Order

1. What the human asked for in this turn
2. `docs/room/` — audience and limits
3. `bits/` and `notes/`
4. Ask the human

## Do not invent

- Laughs, bookings, or that a bit was performed
- A room limit the files do not state
- Someone else's bit, presented as the user's

If the record is silent, say so.
