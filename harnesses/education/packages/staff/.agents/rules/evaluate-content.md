# Evaluate content (agent pointer)

Staff-only (`staff` package). **Install locally; do not commit** to
student-facing trees.

When evaluating a **student submission**, **prompt log**, or **assignment
materials**, use the skill:

`.agents/skills/evaluate-content/SKILL.md`

## Who may use it

| Role | Allowed |
|:-----|:--------|
| Instructor | Yes — full skill, including materials QA |
| TA | Yes — primarily student submissions; scores are drafts for the instructor |

## Quick constraints

- Instructor of record owns final grades; no invented rubrics or chat histories.
- Integrity concerns = hypotheses only; escalate to instructor.
- Prefer non-identifying labels in durable feedback files.
- Generated `FEEDBACK.md` stays local—not for student-facing git.
