# AI course policy (agent-facing)

**Students do not read this file.** Student-facing expectations live in:

`docs/ai-what-to-expect.md` and `docs/ai/*` in the **course repo**
(from the teaching **learner** pack).

## Why this rule exists

Keep a **thin** always-on reminder so student policy is not duplicated (and
drifted) inside faculty `.agents/`.

## Enforce / author against the student doc

- When drafting syllabus blurbs, LMS text, or labs, **align with** the
  course’s `docs/ai-what-to-expect.md` when it is installed.
- Do **not** invent a stricter or looser institutional AI policy than that
  document and the course syllabus.
- If the syllabus and the doc conflict, **syllabus / LMS for that term wins**;
  note the conflict for faculty rather than silently “fixing” published policy.

## Agent-only constraints (not for the student handout)

- Do not put student names, grades, or integrity accusations in committed materials.
- Treat possible academic-integrity issues as **hypotheses for the instructor**,
  not verdicts.
- Never encourage pasting restricted or credentialed data into consumer AI tools.
- Prefer linking students to `docs/ai-what-to-expect.md` over pasting a full
  AI essay into every assignment.

## Course-specific deltas

Student-visible course-only changes: a short section in course `docs/`, or an
overlay package (see `examples/teaching-overlay/`), not a long second policy
under `.agents/`.
