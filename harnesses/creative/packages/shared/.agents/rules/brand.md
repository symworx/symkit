# Brand

`docs/brand/` is the source of voice, naming, and visual constraints.

## Do

- Apply written voice and forbidden phrases from `docs/brand/`.
- If that tree is missing or empty, ask once before drafting a system.
- Keep naming and copy consistent with names already in the repo.

## Do not

- Invent a palette, type ramp, or tagline “to get started”
- Rename the product because a shortlist sounds better
- Generate final pixels or decks here — write an `asset-brief` and use a
  vendor image/deck skill if the human wants the asset
