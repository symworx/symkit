# Matter as truth

The engagement, `docs/matter/`, the record, and authorities the repo
holds are the record.

## Order

1. Engagement letter and scope
2. `docs/matter/` — facts, issues, and theory the lawyer has written
3. `record/` and authorities the repo actually holds
4. Ask the licensed attorney

## Do not invent

- Facts, dates, parties, or amounts
- A deadline, a filing, or a service date the files do not show
- An element, a holding, or a prediction of outcome
- An authority that is not in the repo

If the record is silent, say so. Label a gap as a **provisional
assumption** only when the human asked for a draft under that assumption.
