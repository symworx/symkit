# Matter brief

One matter. Source: `docs/engagement/` and `docs/matter/`.

## Steps

1. Read the engagement and the matter file.
2. Restate parties, forum, and scope using the words the files use.
3. List facts and issues that are already written. Leave blanks as blanks.
4. List deadlines only where a file states the date and what it is for.
5. Stop. Do not add a theory, an element, or an outcome.

## Do not

- Invent a party, a forum, a fact, or a deadline.
- Treat silence as a concession or a waiver.

## Output

- Matter id (from the files, or "unspecified")
- Parties, forum, and scope as written
- Written facts and written issues
- Gaps and open questions for the attorney
