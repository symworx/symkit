# Critique copy

Review words users will read. Pair with `accessibility-review` for structure.

## Steps

1. Identify the artifact and audience.
2. Check claims against `docs/prd/` and `product-truth.md` (no extra promises).
3. Check tone against `docs/brand/` when present.
4. Flag vagueness, stacked hedges, “click here”, and unverifiable superlatives.
5. Offer concrete rewrites, not “make it punchier”.

## Do not

- Invent a new value prop the PRD does not support.
- Grade design layout except where copy depends on it.
- Claim legal or trademark clearance.
