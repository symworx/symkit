# Dad joke

Non-vulgar dad jokes from a premise.

## Steps

1. Read the premise. One joke unless the brief gives a count.
2. Write a short setup, then a punch that is a pun or a too-literal reading of the setup.
3. Stop. Do not add a story around it.
4. If the premise needs vulgarity, sex, or cruelty, say so and do not write the joke.

## Do not

- Use vulgar, sexual, or cruel language.
- Pad one pun into a paragraph.
- Invent laughs, or that the joke was told on stage.

## Output

- Each joke as setup, then punch
- The premise you used
- A premise you refused, if any, and why it is outside a dad joke
